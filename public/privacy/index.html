Privacy & site analytics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand which parts of the journey people enjoy and to find playback problems. It measures visits, referring sites, approximate country, browser and device type, public campaign tags, map interactions, video formats, viewing progress, buffering, playback errors and page performance.

Our analytics sends public media identifiers and numerical measurements. It does not record your screen, microphone, camera, messages, voice recordings or private review feedback. Private review pages are excluded. We do not add advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

Viewing time is approximate. Seeking past a scene does not count as watching it. This Vercel collection honors Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. You can also turn analytics off in this browser below; this preference is saved locally and does not affect video playback.

The custom domain also has Cloudflare performance diagnostics enabled at the CDN. The browser preference above controls our Vercel analytics; it does not control Cloudflare’s separate reporting.
